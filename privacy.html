Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

The short version

SimpleQRStudio does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking, and no analytics run by us. The QR codes you create are made on your iPhone, and what you put in them stays with you. That's the whole idea.

Information we collect

None. The developer of SimpleQRStudio does not collect, store, or receive any personal information from the app. We have no servers that your content passes through and no database of users.

The information you enter

To make a QR code, you type in details such as a website link, WiFi network name and password, contact information, or event details. This information is used only on your device to generate the code you asked for. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Where your designs are stored

Saved designs are stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your designs may also sync through your own private iCloud account so they are available across your devices. That iCloud storage belongs to you and is controlled by your Apple account, the developer has no access to it. You can delete your designs at any time from within the app.

Photos

When you choose to save a QR code, the app adds that image to your Photos library on your device. SimpleQRStudio does not read, browse, or upload the rest of your photo library, it only writes the code you asked it to save.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising software. If you have opted in through your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and aggregate App Store statistics with the developer. This information is provided by Apple, is not tied to your identity, and cannot be used to identify you.

No selling, no tracking

We do not sell personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites. There is nothing to sell, because we do not collect anything in the first place.

This website

This site is a static informational page. It does not require an account, does not run advertising trackers, and does not ask you for personal information.

Children's privacy

SimpleQRStudio is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, email support@simpleqrstudio.com.
