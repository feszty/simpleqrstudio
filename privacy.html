Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

SimpleQRStudio is designed to create QR codes in a straightforward and privacy-conscious way.

Information We Collect

SimpleQRStudio does not require you to create an account. The app is intended to generate QR codes from information you enter into the app, such as website links, contact details, Wi-Fi information, or calendar event details.

QR Code Content

The information you enter to create a QR code is used for the purpose of generating that QR code. SimpleQRStudio does not sell your QR code content.

Analytics and Tracking

SimpleQRStudio does not use third-party advertising trackers. If future versions include optional analytics or diagnostics, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how it is used.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your QR code content with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

QR codes and related data may be stored locally on your device depending on app features and your usage. You can remove locally stored items by deleting them in the app, where supported, or by deleting the app from your device.

Children’s Privacy

SimpleQRStudio is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@simpleqrstudio.com.
