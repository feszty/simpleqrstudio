Support

Need help with SimpleQRStudio? Send an email to support@simpleqrstudio.com.

Common Questions

What can I create with SimpleQRStudio?

You can create QR codes for website links, contact cards, Wi-Fi access, calendar events, and other supported formats.

Do I need an account?

No. SimpleQRStudio is designed to work without requiring an account.

Where is my QR code data stored?

SimpleQRStudio is designed around local QR creation. Some data may be stored locally on your device depending on app features and your usage.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@simpleqrstudio.com and include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue or feature request.
